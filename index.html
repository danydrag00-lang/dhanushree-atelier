Where is Dhanushree Timbers located?

We operate at Shakul Hameed Saw Mill, Anjaneyar Kovil Street, Kuttalam, Kuttalam Taluk, Mayiladuthurai District, Tamil Nadu 609801, India.

What products does Dhanushree Timbers sell?

We offer timber, cut wooden pieces, wooden pallets, doors, door and window frames, and furniture including beds, dining tables, wardrobes, cabinets and more. Availability is confirmed on enquiry.

Can I order customised wooden furniture?

Yes. Share your preferred dimensions, design, timber and finish. We discuss suitability, pricing and timelines before confirming an order.

Does Dhanushree Timbers deliver near Mayiladuthurai?

Local delivery is available within approximately 40 km of Kuttalam. Delivery farther away may be possible depending on availability and charges. Contact us with your PIN code.

How can I get a timber or furniture quotation?

Call or WhatsApp +91 91593 90999, email reachdhanushreetimbers@gmail.com, or use the custom work enquiry form. Share the product, quantity and dimensions you need.

Can I buy wooden doors, frames or pallets in Kuttalam?

Yes, we accept enquiries for wooden doors, door and window frames, wooden pallets and cut timber. Confirm specifications, quantity and availability with our team.

Are the photographs on the website actual stock?

The website photographs are design references and marketing illustrations, not verified current stock or completed Dhanushree work. Ask us about the actual product before ordering.
