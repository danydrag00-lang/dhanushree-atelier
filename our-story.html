A local connection

Dhanushree Timbers & Furnitures serves customers in and around Kuttalam, Mayiladuthurai, with enquiries for timber supplies, wood products, furniture and custom woodwork.

We bring together practical everyday needs and the natural warmth of timber: wooden beds and wardrobes, doors and frames, desks, tables, pallets, cut pieces and more.

Rather than claim that every idea can be manufactured exactly as pictured, we start with an honest conversation about what is needed, what is possible and what the finished item should do.
