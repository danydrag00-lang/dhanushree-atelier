Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Dhanushree Timbers & Furnitures offers furniture, timber and woodwork enquiries in Kuttalam, Mayiladuthurai, Tamil Nadu. Our registered address is listed on the Contact page.

Information you share

If you contact us, you may choose to share your name, contact details, product preferences, dimensions or delivery area. Our custom enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser; filling it in alone does not send the information to us. You decide whether to send the message using WhatsApp.

How we use information

We use information you provide to reply to questions, discuss specifications, prepare quotations, and arrange agreed orders or delivery where applicable. Standard technical information may also be processed by website hosting providers to operate the site.

Third-party services

Our site links to WhatsApp, Instagram, IndiaMART and Google Maps. These services have their own privacy policies. The site also requests fonts from Google Fonts and illustrative images from Pexels, so these services may receive standard technical request information, such as your IP address. We do not operate user accounts or accept online card payments through this site.

Retention and choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you previously provided, subject to applicable legal obligations and business-record requirements. We do not sell customer contact details.
