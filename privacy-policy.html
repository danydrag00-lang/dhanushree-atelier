About this policy

Dhanushree Timbers & Furnitures offers timber, furniture and woodwork enquiries. For enquiries or privacy questions, email reachdhanushreetimbers@gmail.com.

02

Information you provide

You may share your name, phone number, preferred product, measurements, delivery location and other enquiry details by phone, email or WhatsApp. Our custom enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser and does not send information to our website itself.

03

How it is used

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to enquiries, provide quotations, discuss orders and delivery, provide customer support and meet applicable legal or record-keeping requirements.

04

Third-party services

External services, including WhatsApp, Instagram, IndiaMART, GitHub Pages and Google Fonts, may process technical or personal data under their own terms. Google Fonts requests can expose your IP address to Google. Our website does not provide online payment or account registration.

05

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of personal information where applicable, subject to legitimate business and legal record-keeping requirements. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.
