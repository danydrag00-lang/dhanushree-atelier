Website information

The website showcases selected wood products, furniture and custom woodwork offered for enquiry by Dhanushree Timbers & Furnitures. Website information is descriptive and does not establish a binding offer.

03

Quotes and orders

Prices, dimensions, wood type, material finish, customisation, stock availability, manufacturing timelines, delivery charges and payment terms must be confirmed directly before an order is accepted. No website price list or online checkout is provided.

Custom work

Requirements must be discussed and agreed with the business. Minor variations in natural wood grain, colour and markings are expected. Suitability of wood and finish varies by application.

Delivery and service area

Local delivery can be discussed for locations within approximately 40 km of Kuttalam. Delivery beyond that area depends on availability and additional charges. No fixed delivery date is promised on the website.
